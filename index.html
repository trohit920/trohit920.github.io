Uncertainty Quantification for LLMs

PYTORCH · HUGGING FACE · OPEN SOURCE

Token- and sentence-level uncertainty for a local Qwen2.5-1.5B: predictive entropy, perplexity, max-softmax, and semantic entropy via NLI meaning-clustering — benchmarked on factual QA and math.

  • Error-detection AUROC, ECE with reliability diagrams, risk-coverage curves, bootstrap CIs — all hand-implemented and unit-tested.
  • Found a free single forward pass beat 10-sample semantic entropy (0.945 vs 0.900 AUROC), and traced why: question-conditioned NLI collapses on comparative questions.
  • Uncertainty-gated cascade recovered 80% of self-consistency's accuracy gain at 71% of the compute.
